# LIS BY LII — Digital gifting experience

## Brand idea

The product is an intimate bridge: sender → LIS BY LII → recipient. LIS is not
technical infrastructure on screen; it is the house that carries a private note
inside each physical gift. Copy stays emotional on entry screens. Operational
guidance appears only inside the sender creation flow.

## Visual direction

Quiet luxury with editorial spacing, ivory paper and burgundy velvet drawn from
the supplied LIS mark. Champagne gold is used only for small signatures and
ceremonial details. The logo asset is never recoloured, stretched or redrawn.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#F7F0E8` — warm ivory
- Surface: `#FFFDF9` — paper
- Ink: `#261A1D` — near-black wine
- Burgundy: `#6B1530` — primary brand/action
- Deep burgundy: `#4D0B21` — hover and ceremony
- Champagne: `#B99054` — signature/accent
- Blush: `#EAD8D2` — soft depth
- Line: `rgba(84, 46, 56, 0.16)`

Display text uses a high-contrast serif stack with full Vietnamese support;
interface text uses a restrained system sans stack.

## Interaction

- One clear primary action per state.
- Minimum 44px touch targets and visible keyboard focus.
- Motion uses transform/opacity and remains purposeful: hero arrival, card lift,
  and the envelope-opening ritual.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential animation.
- Recipient entry becomes a full-viewport, distraction-free mobile experience.
- Responsive checks target 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px widths.

## Product flow

The shop prints one QR that opens `/open`. Each sender creates a message and
receives a unique six-digit code to write on the physical card. The recipient
scans the QR, enters that code, then opens the matching letter. No account or
login appears in the experience.
